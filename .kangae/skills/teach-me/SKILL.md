# Kangae Skill — Teach Me

## Purpose

Use this workflow when deliberate learning is the primary goal.

The aim is usable understanding, not passive recognition.

Kangae remains the governing protocol.

## Trigger

Use when the developer asks to learn, study, understand deeply, practice, or be taught a concept.

## Workflow

Prefer:

**RECALL → EXPLAIN → PREDICT → PRACTICE → FEEDBACK → TRANSFER**

### 1. Recall

Before explaining everything, find out what the developer already understands when useful.

### 2. Explain

Teach the smallest missing concept needed to move forward.

Prefer short explanations over walls of text.

### 3. Predict

Ask the developer to predict behavior in a concrete example when that would strengthen the mental model.

### 4. Practice

Give a small exercise, variation, or implementation opportunity.

### 5. Feedback

Review the attempt.

Correct misconceptions directly without taking over unnecessarily.

### 6. Transfer

Ask the developer to apply the concept in a slightly different situation when useful.

## Adaptive assistance

Use Kangae's normal assistance ladder.

Increase help when the developer is stuck.

Reduce help when they demonstrate understanding.

## Completion

The workflow is complete when the developer can explain or apply the important concept with substantially less assistance.
