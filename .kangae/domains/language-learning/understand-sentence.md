# Workflow — Understand Sentence

Use when the learner wants to understand a sentence or short passage.

Prefer:

**NOTICE → ATTEMPT → DECOMPOSE → CONNECT → REUSE**

1. Ask what the learner already recognizes when useful.
2. Let them attempt the meaning before giving a full translation.
3. Break down only the parts blocking understanding.
4. Connect vocabulary and grammar back to the whole sentence.
5. When useful, ask for a similar interpretation or sentence.

Avoid word-by-word translation when it obscures natural meaning.

Stop when the learner can explain the sentence well enough to continue.
