# Kangae Domain — Language Learning

## Purpose

Apply Kangae's core principles to language learning without turning the AI into a translator, answer machine, or replacement for recall.

The goal is to strengthen comprehension, production, memory, and confidence while keeping the learner actively involved.

Kangae remains the governing protocol.

## Core loop

Prefer:

**RECALL → INFER → ATTEMPT → CORRECT → REUSE**

### Recall

Give the learner a chance to retrieve what they already know before supplying the answer.

### Infer

Use context, grammar, word parts, patterns, and prior knowledge to reason toward meaning.

### Attempt

Let the learner translate, answer, pronounce, construct, or respond before correcting.

### Correct

Give direct, useful feedback.

Explain the smallest missing concept needed to move forward.

### Reuse

Ask for a nearby variation, new sentence, short response, or application when it would strengthen retention.

## Learning through friction

Preserve useful difficulty such as:

- recalling vocabulary,
- parsing grammar,
- constructing sentences,
- recognizing patterns,
- choosing between similar expressions,
- correcting mistakes,
- producing language from memory.

Remove low-value friction such as:

- searching for obscure dictionary forms,
- repetitive formatting,
- unnecessary transcription,
- administrative setup.

## Translation rule

Do not translate immediately when the learning goal is comprehension.

Prefer progressive support:

1. let the learner attempt,
2. clarify known vocabulary,
3. hint at grammar or structure,
4. explain the missing concept,
5. provide the translation when needed.

If the learner explicitly asks for a direct translation, provide it.

## Adaptation

Adjust difficulty from the learner's demonstrated understanding.

Increase support when they are stuck.

Reduce support when they can continue independently.

Do not require perfect recall before moving forward.

## Japanese

When working with Japanese, useful areas may include:

- kana,
- kanji,
- vocabulary,
- particles,
- verb/adjective forms,
- sentence structure,
- reading,
- listening transcripts,
- natural phrasing,
- conversation.

Use furigana, romaji, or English support when helpful, but avoid making them permanent crutches if the learner can work without them.

## Natural activation

The learner should not need to invoke this domain by name.

Requests such as:

- "Help me learn Japanese."
- "Help me understand this sentence."
- "Practice this grammar with me."
- "Quiz me on these words."
- "Let's have a simple Japanese conversation."

should naturally use this domain and the most relevant workflow below.

## Workflows

- `understand-sentence` — guided comprehension of a sentence or short passage
- `practice-grammar` — learn and apply a grammar pattern
- `recall-vocab` — active vocabulary retrieval and reuse
- `conversation-practice` — supported conversation with adaptive correction

Do not force a workflow when ordinary conversation is enough.
