# Stage 06 — Reflect

## Purpose

Turn meaningful completed work into reusable learning without creating busywork.

## Reads

- relevant outputs from earlier stages
- `JOURNAL-TEMPLATE.md`
- `KANGAE.md`
- `skills/reconstruct/SKILL.md` when transfer should be verified
- `skills/journal/SKILL.md` when a journal entry is useful

## Responsibilities

Capture only useful, reviewable artifacts such as:

- the problem
- initial understanding
- important hypotheses
- attempts that mattered
- evidence that changed the direction
- final decision
- reusable lesson
- remaining uncertainty
- what to try differently next time

Kangae applies here through proof of understanding.

When AI assistance was substantial and the concept matters, use a light reconstruction or transfer check before treating recognition as mastery.

Do not reconstruct hidden chain-of-thought or invent motivations.

## Produces

Usually:

`output/journal.md`

Reflection may be skipped entirely when the work produced little reusable learning.

## Checkpoint

Reflection is complete when:

- the useful lesson is captured,
- uncertainty is preserved,
- and the developer has reviewed or corrected the artifact when needed.

Do not prolong the session after sufficient understanding has been demonstrated.

## Routes to

Usually:

- completion
- `02_think` or a Teach Me/Reconstruct skill when reflection reveals an important gap
