# Stage 06 — Reflect

## Goal

Turn the completed work into reusable learning.

## Inputs

- relevant outputs from earlier stages
- `JOURNAL-TEMPLATE.md`
- `KANGAE.md`

## Process

Summarize only reviewable, observable work.

Capture:

- the problem
- initial understanding
- important hypotheses
- what was tried
- evidence that mattered
- what changed the mental model
- the final decision
- reusable learning
- anything still unclear
- what to try next time

Do not invent hidden reasoning or reconstruct private chain-of-thought.

## Output

Write a draft to:

`output/journal.md`

The developer reviews and corrects the entry before treating it as final.
